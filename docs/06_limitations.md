# 06 · Limitations

Stated plainly, because softening them invites reviewers to sharpen them.

1. **No demand validation.** The composite index is a proxy; nothing here shows it predicts ridership.
   Driver GPS carries no ridership signal. The expert panel (V3) and an on-street boarding survey were
   **not conducted**.
2. **The operator benchmark is circular and fails.** CHALO aggregates both calibrate a plausibility term
   and serve as benchmark V2, and the engine floors the backbone fleet at CHALO's deployment on 2 routes.
   The plan still exceeds the frequency-scaled CHALO fleet by 29–58 %.
3. **Population is modelled.** WorldPop 2026 is anchored on the 2011 census and sits 4.4 % below it.
4. **Observed pace is Srinagar-weighted.** The observation-anchored fleet uses 16 corridors near the city;
   rural pace is unobserved, so the rural fleet is as modelled in every scenario.
5. **Rural headways inherit the demand proxy.** On 67 rural routes the engine sets headway from the
   quarantined demand estimate.
6. **No depot register, no cost records.** Deadhead is bounded, not measured; cost and emission constants
   are unverified ranges.
7. **Before/after equity is not computable.** Merged permits carry no geometry, so only the after-state
   Gini and the named losers are reported.
8. **Behaviour is static.** No mode shift or induced demand is modelled; the day-one load result is a
   requirement, not a forecast.
9. **One region.** Transferability to other permit-based cities is argued from preconditions, not
   demonstrated.
